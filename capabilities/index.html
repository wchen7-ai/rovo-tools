Answer these four before you answer the customer

1
Built-in AI or an agent?
Embedded AI = point-and-click, in-product. Agents = cross-product teammates (OOTB or built in Studio).
2
GA, beta, or roadmap?
Don't promise beta as shipped. Focus's Strategic Intelligence is beta; Assets & Analytics need a live check.
3
Do they have the edition?
Full Rovo needs Premium/Enterprise Cloud. Strategy needs Jira Premium. Free gets nothing.
4
What will it cost?
Credits ≠ tokens. ~10/request, pooled org-wide, monthly reset. Only 4 subscriptions fund the pool.
